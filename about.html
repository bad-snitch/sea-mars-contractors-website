Who We Are

Sea Mars Contractors Nig. Ltd. is a trusted partner in marine consultancy and vessel support.

We are a team of experienced marine professionals committed to delivering practical, accountable solutions for vessel maintenance, dry docking operations, and marine consultancy. Our approach is rooted in industry experience, regulatory understanding, and a genuine commitment to quality service delivery.

Since our inception, we have worked with operators, shipyards, and fleet managers to ensure smooth, compliant, and efficient vessel maintenance operations across the maritime sector.
